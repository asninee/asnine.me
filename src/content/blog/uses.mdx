## Software

<SectionList>
  <SectionListItem
    badge='editor'
    title='Zed'
    href='https://zed.dev'
    desc='With my agentic coding setup, being able to have a minimal but performant editor that quickly gets out of the way is a necessity. And so, Zed couldn’t really be a better fit for me, with it’s more-than-good-enough extension ecosystem.'
  />
  <SectionListItem
    badge='terminal'
    title='Ghostty'
    href='https://ghostty.org'
    desc='It’s blazing fast (it uses Zig under the hood), allows me to blitz through any commands/scripts that I need to run, and has a fantastic community behind it.'
  />
  <SectionListItem
    badge='browser'
    title='Helium'
    href='https://helium.computer'
    desc='This takes Chromium, trims all the fat, but also has a intriguing code-patching approach which means that the dev team can be selective in what they want to incorporate (or apply a refined spin of), which lends to it’s resource-efficient and hardened nature.'
  />
  <SectionListItem
    badge='launcher'
    title='Tuna'
    href='https://tunaformac.com'
    desc='This is a unique launcher that comes with a variety of modes such that you can; search for apps/files/actions, create your own keybinds for rapidly running said apps/actions, add extensions (ala Raycast) to extend the things that you can do.'
  />
  <SectionListItem
    badge='passwords'
    title='1Password'
    href='https://1password.com'
    desc='Comes with an integrated SSH agent (which is very handy). It was the first offering to provide Passkeys support (iirc), and comes with this nice "Watchtower" feature where you can see how strong your passwords are collectively.'
  />
  <SectionListItem
    badge='notes'
    title='Obsidian'
    href='https://obsidian.md'
    desc='Love the local-first approach, the wide variety of extensions available, and how you can structure your notes the way you want to (this came in very clutch during my uni years).'
  />
  <SectionListItem
    badge='misc'
    title='Swish'
    href='https://highlyopinionated.co/swish'
    desc='A neat window-manager app that leverages trackpad gestures (after this, wanting to own a wireless mouse becomes very undesirable - this didn’t age well, but it’s one of those occasional pleasures I enjoy when I can use my mac undocked).'
  />
  <SectionListItem
    badge='misc'
    title='BetterDisplay'
    href='https://betterdisplay.pro'
    desc='A display management utility that allows you to convert external displays to use Apple’s HiDPI, which gives you significantly sharper text. Hook this up nicely with a resolution that gives you enough screen estate (while still being crisp), and you’ve got a winner.'
  />
  <SectionListItem
    badge='misc'
    title='Clop'
    href='https://lowtechguys.com/clop'
    desc='An image, video, pdf and clipboard optimiser, which I’ve set up to run upon a file being copied/downloaded.'
  />
  <SectionListItem
    badge='misc'
    title='Alcove'
    href='https://tryalcove.com'
    desc='An app that emulates the functionality of the Dynamic Island on iPhones, and brings it over to the Mac. It’s continued to get better over time to the point that it can house calendar events, among other things, allowing me to remove other redundant apps.'
  />
  <SectionListItem
    badge='misc'
    title='Dropover'
    href='https://dropoverapp.com'
    desc='An app that makes drag and drop very easy, with you being able to group items into stashes.'

</SectionList>
